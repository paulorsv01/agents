# Grilling

Reach a shared understanding of the decision, its trade-offs, and unresolved assumptions.

## Interview

Ask one decision question at a time, give your recommendation, and wait for the answer.
Resolve dependencies before dependent decisions. Investigate facts available in the environment directly.
Focus questions on choices that affect the plan; preserve decisions already settled in the conversation.

## Completion

The interview is complete when consequential questions are resolved and the user confirms the shared understanding.
Present the resulting decision and any remaining uncertainty. Begin implementation when the user authorizes that next phase.
